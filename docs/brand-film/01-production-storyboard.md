## Motion language

- Primary easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` to match the portfolio’s existing ease-out token.
- Use 6–10 frame dissolves only for conceptual transitions; product interactions use clean cuts.
- Cursor scale: 32–40 px at 1080p, with a 2-frame press compression to 92%.
- Interface zooms: 100%→104% maximum. Avoid cinematic parallax that distorts product truth.
- Text entrance: 8 px upward move + opacity over 8–10 frames.
- Maintain at least 18 frames of uninterrupted visibility for every primary title.
